1. Introduction

Welcome to Vespera, a Pomodoro focus timer application designed to help users manage their focus sessions, breaks, daily productivity goals, and personal productivity activities.

This Privacy Policy explains how Vespera handles information when you use the application. It describes what information is stored, how it is used, where it is stored, and how it is handled.

Vespera is intended for users of all ages. By using the application, you acknowledge that you have read this Privacy Policy.

2. Information We Handle

Vespera allows users to create and manage information related to their focus activities and application preferences.

Depending on how you use the application, the following information may be stored locally on your device:

  • Focus session history and session records.
  • Selected activity categories, such as Study, Work, Writing, Reading, Exercise, Wellness, and Meditation.
  • Custom activity categories created by users.
  • Focus session duration.
  • Short break and long break duration.
  • Daily focus goals and session targets.
  • Theme preferences.
  • Music preferences and application settings.
  • Notification and vibration preferences.

This information is used to provide the application's features and maintain your preferences.

Vespera does not require users to create an account, register, or sign in to use its core features.

3. Local Data Storage

Vespera stores application-related information locally on your device.

Your focus history, activity categories, custom categories, statistics, settings, and preferences are stored in the application's local storage.

Vespera does not operate its own cloud storage or account synchronization service.

Application data is not intended to be uploaded to a developer-operated server or synchronized across devices.

Vespera does not provide a cloud backup or cloud restoration feature. Application data is intended to remain on your device and is not intended to be restored from a cloud backup.

4. How We Use Information

Information stored by Vespera is used only to provide and improve the application's functionality.

Specifically, information may be used to:

  • Start, manage, and track focus sessions.
  • Manage short breaks and long breaks.
  • Maintain focus session history.
  • Display productivity statistics and activity summaries.
  • Calculate daily focus goal progress.
  • Remember selected and custom activity categories.
  • Save application preferences and theme selections.
  • Maintain music, notification, and vibration preferences.
  • Support timer notifications and session completion alerts.

Vespera does not use locally stored productivity information to create user advertising profiles.

5. Activity Categories and Custom Categories

Vespera provides predefined activity categories to help users organize their focus sessions.

Users may also create their own custom activity categories.

When a user selects an activity category during a focus session, the selected category may be saved with the corresponding session history and used to organize productivity statistics.

Custom category names and related session information are stored locally on the user's device.

6. Data Sharing and Disclosure

Vespera does not operate its own user account system or developer-operated cloud database for storing users' focus history and application preferences.

Vespera does not sell users' locally stored productivity information.

Vespera does not intentionally share locally stored focus history, custom activity categories, or application preferences with third parties.

However, third-party services may process certain information when they are integrated into the application. Details about advertising services are explained in the following section.

8. Notifications and Device Permissions

Vespera provides timer-related notifications to help users keep track of active focus sessions and breaks.

Depending on the device and Android version, the application may request permission to display notifications.

Notifications may be used to:

  • Display information about an active focus session or break.
  • Inform users when a focus session or break has finished.
  • Notify users about automatically continuing sessions, where enabled.

Vespera may also use the device's vibration functionality when a focus session or break is completed, depending on the user's preferences.

The application uses the relevant Android notification and vibration capabilities to provide these features.

Vespera's built-in music functionality uses audio included within the application. It does not require users to provide personal music files or connect an external music streaming account.

9. Data Retention and Deletion

Application information is retained locally on the device while it is needed to provide Vespera's functionality.

Vespera does not provide a developer-operated cloud account from which users' focus history or preferences can be retrieved.

When the application is uninstalled, its locally stored application data is intended to be deleted by Android along with the application.

If the application is installed again, it starts as a new installation with its default settings. Previous application data is not intended to be restored.

Please note that Android device backup or transfer behavior may depend on the application's final backup configuration and device settings.

10. Children's Privacy

Vespera is intended for a general audience and may be used by people of different ages.

Vespera does not require users to provide their names, email addresses, phone numbers, or other personal details to access its core features.

The application is designed to store productivity information locally on the user's device.

If advertising services are introduced, appropriate safeguards, advertising settings, and consent mechanisms will be implemented as required by applicable laws and Google Play's Families Policy.

Parents or legal guardians who have questions about the application's privacy practices may contact the developer using the contact information provided below.

11. Data Security

Vespera uses Android's application storage environment to store locally maintained application information.

The developer aims to protect application information through the security mechanisms provided by the Android platform and appropriate application development practices.

However, no method of electronic storage or software-based security can guarantee absolute security.

Users are encouraged to keep their devices protected with appropriate device security measures.
